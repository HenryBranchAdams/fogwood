# Compare & Decide

Compare & Decide is a local, immutable recipe definition for comparing Alpha and Beta with typed criteria, bounded weights, and a reviewable scorecard. Its recipe names trusted host-owned instrument and behavior IDs; it does not supply their implementation.

The recipe previews the exact current staged composition: 12 page blocks and the local Compare instrument projection of 10 typed instances connected by 12 one-way bindings. Its data-only projection preserves the fixture's stable identities, typed ports, formula ASTs, and default result of Alpha 74, Beta 78, recommendation Beta. A person remains the decision authority. Scores are derived aids, not conclusions.

The included examples and fixtures describe the bounded Alpha/Beta inputs, malformed and oversized requests, a successful 74/78/Beta fixture, and an information-loss warning case.
