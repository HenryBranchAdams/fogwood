# Compare & Decide skill

## Intent

Choose this package when a person needs to compare local alternatives, expose tradeoffs, and keep the decision boundary reviewable.

## Workflow

1. Inspect the current page revision and exposure policy.
2. Read the pinned package identity, six typed input ports, four output ports, and the recipe example.
3. Ask for Alpha and Beta, cost and impact weights, bounded scores, and what would change the decision.
4. Stage the immutable recipe against the inspected revision.
5. Explain that the fixture's 74/78/Beta output is a bounded aid, then wait for page-owned Apply or Reject.

## Review checks

- Every criterion has a visible meaning and bounded input.
- The 10-instance, 12-binding graph remains typed, one-way, and acyclic.
- Derived results disclose their inputs and remain deterministic.
- Missing or stale values remain visibly unresolved.
- A score never replaces the person's judgment.

## Prohibited effects

Do not retrieve alternatives, assign a winner, hide a missing value, or apply a proposal without the person's page action.
