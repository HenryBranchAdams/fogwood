# Evidence Map skill

## Intent

Choose this package when a question needs a visible relationship between local sources, narrow claims, counterevidence, and unresolved work.

## Workflow

1. Inspect the current page revision and exposure policy.
2. Read the pinned package identity and the recipe example.
3. Ask for the topic, source labels, and the claims that need review.
4. Stage the immutable recipe against the inspected revision.
5. Explain what the map cannot verify, then wait for page-owned Apply or Reject.

## Review checks

- Source labels remain distinct from interpretation.
- Each claim is narrow enough to review.
- Counterevidence and open questions have visible space.
- No catalog material is treated as executable authority.

## Prohibited effects

Do not retrieve a source, infer a citation, publish the board, or apply a proposal without the person's page action.
