# Meeting Wall skill

## Intent

Choose this package when local meeting material needs a visible handoff from context to decisions, risks, and owned next actions.

## Workflow

1. Inspect the current page revision and exposure policy.
2. Read the pinned package identity and the recipe example.
3. Ask for the meeting title, decisions, risks, and actions that need owners.
4. Stage the immutable recipe against the inspected revision.
5. Explain unassigned or uncertain fields, then wait for page-owned Apply or Reject.

## Review checks

- Notes remain distinct from decisions.
- Each action has one clear next move.
- Risks have a visible response space.
- Unassigned owners remain explicit rather than guessed.

## Prohibited effects

Do not capture audio, infer attendance, assign a person, send a message, or apply a proposal without the person's page action.
